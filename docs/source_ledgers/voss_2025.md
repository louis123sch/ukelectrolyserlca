## §3.1.1.2 — natural graphite (a factor, not a table) → `bess_natural_graphite_1kg`

ESI p. 9, verbatim: Engels et al.³¹ "determined a CF of 9.62 kg CO2-eq kg⁻¹ NGr … we
subtracted the impact of the coating step … we reduced the impact of spheronization by
25 %, applying economic allocation … Moreover, we added the transport from China to
Europe. This results in a total CF of 5.19 kg CO2-eq kg⁻¹ NGr."

| # | Source row | Amount | Unit | Module (key, amount) | Disposition | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NGr carbon footprint, Engels adjusted (+ transport, as the sentence reads) | 5.19 | kg CO2-eq kg⁻¹ | `co2` 5.19 (`NGR_KGCO2E_PER_KG`), then `sea_container` −18.5 t·km and `lorry_euro3_rer` −0.7 t·km; built ≈ 4.87 kg CO2e kg⁻¹ | IN-DIFFERS (5.19 pinned; Voß's value in the cell is 5.7) | **The main text gives NGr at 5.7 kg CO2-eq kg⁻¹** (Fig. 6 and §4.2.2, p. 8118–8119; Conclusions, p. 8122). The anode-composite figures on p. 8118 tie to it: 100 % NGr anode 5.9 kg kWh⁻¹, less CMC + SBR + carbon black ≈ 0.15 kg kWh⁻¹ (at Voß's 4.8, 2.5, ~2.5 kg kg⁻¹), over 1.011 kg AAM kWh⁻¹ = 5.68; the same arithmetic on 25.5 gives 25.07 ≈ 25.1 for SGr; and the 75:25 blend 0.75×25.1 + 0.25×5.7 at 1.011 + 0.15 = 20.63 ≈ their 20.6. Since 25.1 includes the three legs (SOURCES.md's own rebuild) and 5.19 + 0.51 = 5.70 exactly, **5.19 is almost certainly the Engels-adjusted figure before transport and 5.7 the figure with it**, the ESI sentence notwithstanding. The module therefore subtracts two legs (−0.32 kg CO2e kg⁻¹) from a number that does not contain them, and omits the China leg it means to keep (+0.19). Under-count 0.51 kg CO2e kg⁻¹ NGr × 0.2527 kg NGr kWh⁻¹ = **−0.13 kg CO2e kWh⁻¹** on the cell (0.16 %), opposite in sign to the 21 Oct restore. Grade: likely (three independent main-text numbers agree; only the ESI sentence order says otherwise). Fix: pin 5.19 with no negative exchanges and add +1.0 t·km `lorry_euro3_row`, like every other material (built 5.38). SOURCES.md's "4.675 kg/kg, +0.515 transport = 5.19" is a back-solve from the same misreading. |
