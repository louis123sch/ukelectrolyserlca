## Claims to check

1. **"Leng publish NO factory energy for either transformer" — confirmed.** Table S1 (SI p2) is materials only: for each transformer five rows (Silicon dioxide, Steel, Copper, Iron, Transformer oil) and nothing else; S2 is transport; S3 is construction; S4 O&M; S5 EoL. No energy row, kWh or MJ, appears for a transformer anywhere in the SI. Main text §2.2.1 (p5–6): "inventory data for key components (notably turbines and transformers) were supplemented using publicly available literature … the main transformer was modeled based on published studies [33]" — Li, Li & Wu (2020), not held. Table 3 (p10) labels both transformers' second-order process "Materials/Construction Machinery", but no machinery or energy quantity is printed for them. Fig. 6 (p11) gives only the Stage I split (Vestas 52.1 %, Goldwind 43.6 %, main transformer 0.7 %, box 3.6 %) and the turbines' material pies. The module's and SOURCES.md's statement stands.

2. **"Steel and Iron are listed separately" — confirmed** for all four S1 blocks (SI p2): both turbines and both transformers have a Steel row and an Iron row. Leng never say what "Iron" is. One new piece of evidence the module should weigh: Table S5 (SI p6) classes the farm's end-of-life metals as "Metal material: Steel / **Cast iron** / Copper", with no other iron category. Together with the turbine blocks (where "Iron" is the Vestas LCA's cast-iron hub and mainframe), this makes it likely Leng scored "Iron" on a cast-iron dataset for the transformers too. The module's core-steel reading is still the physically better one for a transformer, and its effect is stated (~4 %), but it is a departure from the source's own treatment, not a neutral reading of it — the ASSUMED note could say so.

3. **Box-transformer rating: 3,500 kVA vs 2.2 MVA — the contradiction is real.** Main p8: "Each wind turbine tower is connected to a 3500 KVA box-type transformer (S11-2200/35, JULI, Suzhou, China)". In the Chinese designation S11-2200/35 the middle field is the rated kVA (2,200) and the suffix the HV kV (35); Table S1 (SI p2) and p8 both print the model number.
