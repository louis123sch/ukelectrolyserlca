## Why each one is here

**electrolyser embodied carbon** — Guards the decomposition every hydrogen result depends on.

**hydrogen round-trip efficiency** — A chain-wide sanity check on the efficiencies and unit conversions.
  3.41 kWh in per kWh out. This is the number that decides whether grid-charged hydrogen arbitrage can pay.

**grid_only reproduces baseline_grid.py** — Two unrelated routes to the same number; disagreement means one is wrong.
  Published baseline is 11,150 t/yr at 7.3063 MW, including a 32.5 t/yr genset the LP excludes.

**Pareto ordering** — The defining property of the two anchors. Failure means the sweep is wrong.

**grid-charged hydrogen arbitrage** — An analytic prediction the LP can confirm or contradict independently.
  Spread below penalty and nothing built — the prediction and the optimiser agree.

**islanded with no generation** — A degenerate case that must fail loudly and legibly.

**free zero-carbon grid** — The opposite degenerate case. Anything built means a term is mis-signed.

**delivered cost per MWh** — A units and annualisation smell test, not a precise benchmark.
  Grid-only delivered is £103.09/MWh including network charges.

**superset control** — Catches divergent bounds or coefficients between architectures.

**the optimiser's choice of price list** — The switched model (COST_PLAN §1.11) must contain the both-ways comparison it replaces.
  Forced local run capped at the company's 240 MVA; the switched run adds the smaller bands and voltages.

## What these do not cover

* **Perfect foresight.** Every result assumes the whole window is known in
  advance. The numbers are an upper bound on achievable performance, not a
  forecast. Running one configuration against notebook 4.3's myopic merit
  order would bracket the truth; that has not been done.
* **Cost inputs for the hydrogen chain.** Fuel cell and vessel capex are
  assumed with no UK source and spreads of 4.5x and 2x. No benchmark here
  can validate them — see `DATA_GAPS.md`.
* **Seven regions, one year.** Full-year grid series are cached for North
  Scotland, the North West, Yorkshire, the East Midlands, the South West, London
  and the South East; the other regions hold only the June week, so a full-year
  run there builds its grid series first.
* **No unit commitment.** The LP may run an electrolyser at 1 % of rating.
